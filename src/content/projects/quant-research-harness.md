## What it is
A research platform designed to keep data, factor definitions, evaluations, experiment metadata, and model-search workflows under one coherent interface.

## Design principle
The workbench owns durable research state. Agents are bounded orchestrators rather than opaque autonomous systems, and search algorithms remain replaceable plugins.

## Current focus
Improving memory efficiency, factor-library promotion workflows, correlation de-duplication, experiment lineage, and interfaces that are equally legible to researchers and coding agents.
