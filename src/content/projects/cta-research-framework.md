## Scope
A reusable research framework for CTA-style signals, with shared feature construction, position mapping, risk controls, and out-of-sample diagnostics.

## Strategy families
Trend, mean reversion, breakout, volatility state, cross-sectional relative strength, and event-conditioned variants can be compared under the same execution assumptions.

## Research discipline
The framework emphasizes stability across markets and regimes rather than optimizing one headline backtest statistic.
