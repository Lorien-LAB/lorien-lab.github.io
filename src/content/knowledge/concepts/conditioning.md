## Core idea

Condition on information that turns a complicated random structure into simpler cases, then recombine the cases with the law of total probability or total expectation.

## Recognition pattern

Look for a natural first event, hidden state, branch, or partial observation that makes the remaining calculation easier.

## Common trap

Conditioning is useful only when the conditioned cases are simpler and the conditional probabilities or expectations can be computed consistently.
