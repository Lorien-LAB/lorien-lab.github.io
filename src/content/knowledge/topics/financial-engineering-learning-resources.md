> **Source acknowledgement.** This learning map is adapted from **[Prof. Chuan Shi (石川教授)](https://www.shichuan.info/)**'s *A Curated Guide to Financial Engineering Resources* (November 2025). The resource selection, descriptions, and recommended learning path originate from Prof. Shi's guide; Lorien Lab restructures them as connected Knowledge Base entries and adds verified official links for navigation.

## About this guide

Prof. Shi's guide collects online resources intended to accelerate learning in quantitative finance and organizes them into a practical progression from core skills to factor investing, advanced machine learning, system building, and frontier research.

The descriptions below preserve the guide's framing. They are a structured index of the resources it recommends rather than an independent ranking or verification of those resources. This Lorien Lab adaptation is not authored by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Prof. Shi.

## 1. Core Skills

- **[WorldQuant University](/knowledge/worldquant-university/)** — project-based data science and deep-learning labs covering the data-science workflow, neural networks, and computer-vision applications.
- **[QuantStart](/knowledge/quantstart/)** — tutorials spanning quantitative trading and investing, with an emphasis on practical programming skills and real-world trading systems.

## 2. Factor Investing

- **[Machine Learning for Factor Investing](/knowledge/machine-learning-for-factor-investing/)** — a free book with code and data combining modern machine learning with traditional factor-investing approaches.
- **[DDA3600 Factor Investing Course Materials](/knowledge/dda3600-factor-investing-course-materials/)** — university course materials providing theoretical foundations and an academic framework for factor investing.
- **[AQR Insights](/knowledge/aqr-insights/)** — industry research and commentary highlighted by the guide for macroeconomic perspective and real-world factor applications.

## 3. Advanced Concepts

- **[Advances in Financial Machine Learning](/knowledge/advances-in-financial-machine-learning/)** — exercises associated with Marcos López de Prado's work, emphasizing common pitfalls in financial data analysis and advanced financial-ML methodologies.
- **[Interpretable Machine Learning](/knowledge/interpretable-machine-learning/)** — a free book focused on model interpretability and the role of interpretability in reliable model deployment.

## 4. Systems

- **[QuantConnect LEAN](/knowledge/quantconnect-lean/)** — an open-source algorithmic-trading engine highlighted for studying backtesting, event-driven architecture, and asset modeling in real systems.

## 5. Stay Current

- **[AI & Big Data in Finance Research Forum](/knowledge/ai-big-data-finance-research-forum/)** — webinars connecting academic research with industry practice and helping readers follow developments at the academic-industry frontier.

## Recommended learning path

1. Start with **Core Skills** through [WorldQuant University](/knowledge/worldquant-university/) and [QuantStart](/knowledge/quantstart/).
2. Specialize in **Factor Investing** through [Machine Learning for Factor Investing](/knowledge/machine-learning-for-factor-investing/), [DDA3600](/knowledge/dda3600-factor-investing-course-materials/), and [AQR Insights](/knowledge/aqr-insights/).
3. Study **Advanced Concepts** through [Advances in Financial Machine Learning](/knowledge/advances-in-financial-machine-learning/) and [Interpretable Machine Learning](/knowledge/interpretable-machine-learning/).
4. Get hands-on with **Systems** through [QuantConnect LEAN](/knowledge/quantconnect-lean/).
5. **Stay Current** through the [AI & Big Data in Finance Research Forum](/knowledge/ai-big-data-finance-research-forum/).
