## About this guide

This learning map is based on Chuan Shi's *A Curated Guide to Financial Engineering Resources* (November 2025). The guide collects free online resources intended to accelerate learning in quantitative finance and organizes them into a practical progression from core skills to factor investing, advanced machine learning, system building, and frontier research.

The descriptions below preserve the guide's framing. They are a structured index of the resources it recommends rather than an independent ranking or verification of those resources.

## 1. Core Skills

- **WorldQuant University** — project-based data science and deep-learning labs covering the data-science workflow, neural networks, and computer-vision applications.
- **QuantStart** — tutorials spanning quantitative trading and investing, with an emphasis on practical programming skills and real-world trading systems.

## 2. Factor Investing

- **Machine Learning for Factor Investing** — a free book with code and data combining modern machine learning with traditional factor-investing approaches.
- **DDA3600 Factor Investing Course Materials** — university course materials providing theoretical foundations and an academic framework for factor investing.
- **AQR Insights** — industry research and commentary highlighted by the guide for macroeconomic perspective and real-world factor applications.

## 3. Advanced Concepts

- **Advances in Financial Machine Learning** — exercises associated with Marcos López de Prado's work, emphasizing common pitfalls in financial data analysis and advanced financial-ML methodologies.
- **Interpretable Machine Learning** — a free book focused on model interpretability and the role of interpretability in reliable model deployment.

## 4. Systems

- **QuantConnect LEAN** — an open-source algorithmic-trading engine highlighted for studying backtesting, event-driven architecture, and asset modeling in real systems.

## 5. Stay Current

- **AI & Big Data in Finance Research Forum** — webinars connecting academic research with industry practice and helping readers follow developments at the academic-industry frontier.

## Recommended learning path

1. Start with **Core Skills** through WorldQuant University and QuantStart.
2. Specialize in **Factor Investing** through Machine Learning for Factor Investing, DDA3600, and AQR Insights.
3. Study **Advanced Concepts** through Advances in Financial Machine Learning and Interpretable Machine Learning.
4. Get hands-on with **Systems** through QuantConnect LEAN.
5. **Stay Current** through the AI & Big Data in Finance Research Forum.
